# Client Delivery Notes

## What to Deliver to a Business Owner

### 1. Strategy Summary

Explain:

- What audience was targeted
- Which topic cluster was selected
- Why each article exists
- How articles connect

### 2. Content Pack

Provide:

- Pillar article
- Supporting articles
- Metadata
- Internal-link plan
- Suggested CTA

### 3. Client Inputs Needed

Request:

- Correct business name
- Exact service list
- Service area
- Contact details
- Current prices, if they want prices published
- Approved credentials
- Photos/assets
- Brand voice
- Expert reviewer

### 4. Important Note

AI-generated content should be reviewed before publication. The client is responsible for confirming business-specific facts and claims.

## Suggested Service Package

A freelancer or agency could package the workflow as:

- Monthly keyword mapping
- 1 pillar + 3–5 supporting articles
- Internal linking
- Local SEO adaptation
- Metadata
- Monthly content report

Pricing should be determined based on market, scope, expertise, research depth and client requirements.
