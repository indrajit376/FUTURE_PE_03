# SEO Production Workflow

## Phase 1 — Discovery

Collect:

- Business name
- Website
- Services
- Service area
- Target customers
- Conversion goal
- Existing pages
- Brand tone
- Expert reviewer

## Phase 2 — Keyword Mapping

Use the keyword-intent prompt.

Validate candidate keywords using current SEO tools. Review the actual SERP to understand:

- Search intent
- Content formats
- Common questions
- Local results
- Competitor page angles
- Missing useful information

## Phase 3 — Content Architecture

Choose one pillar topic and several supporting topics.

Avoid creating multiple pages that target the same intent.

## Phase 4 — Drafting

Generate the outline first, then the article.

Use AI to accelerate the first draft but maintain human editorial control.

## Phase 5 — Optimization

Check:

- Intent
- Title
- Headings
- Topic coverage
- Readability
- Internal links
- Local relevance
- CTA
- Metadata

## Phase 6 — Fact Checking

Verify all:

- Statistics
- Prices
- Dates
- Credentials
- Medical/legal/financial claims
- Product specifications
- Citations

## Phase 7 — Publication

Publish only after business-owner approval and subject-matter review where required.

## Phase 8 — Measurement

Track relevant metrics such as:

- Organic impressions
- Clicks
- CTR
- Queries
- Landing-page engagement
- Qualified enquiries

Do not promise rankings or traffic outcomes.
