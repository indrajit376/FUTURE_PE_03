# Prompt 04 — Supporting Blog Generator

Create a supporting article that belongs to a larger SEO topic cluster.

PILLAR TOPIC:
[PILLAR TOPIC]

SUPPORTING TOPIC:
[SUPPORTING TOPIC]

PRIMARY KEYWORD:
[PRIMARY KEYWORD]

SEARCH INTENT:
[INTENT]

AUDIENCE:
[AUDIENCE]

LOCATION:
[LOCATION]

The article must:
- Fully answer the narrower question.
- Avoid repeating the entire pillar article.
- Add genuinely useful detail.
- Link naturally to the pillar.
- Link to 1–2 other relevant supporting articles when useful.
- Use a clear H1-H3 structure.
- Include a useful CTA.
- Avoid keyword stuffing.
- Avoid fabricated statistics or unsupported claims.
- Mark uncertain factual statements for review.

Output:
Title, meta description suggestion, outline, article body, FAQ, internal links and fact-check notes.
