# Prompt 06 — Local SEO Adapter

Adapt this article for a local business:

BUSINESS TYPE:
[BUSINESS]

CITY:
[CITY]

NEIGHBORHOODS/SERVICE AREA:
[AREAS]

SERVICE:
[SERVICE]

ARTICLE:
[PASTE ARTICLE]

Requirements:
- Add useful local context naturally.
- Use [CITY] + [SERVICE] only where it reads naturally.
- Do not stuff city names into every heading.
- Do not fabricate local statistics, landmarks, reviews or service claims.
- Include a locally relevant CTA.
- Suggest title tag and meta description.
- Suggest local FAQ questions.
- Suggest internal links to service/contact/location pages.
- Preserve helpfulness and readability.
