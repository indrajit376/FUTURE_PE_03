# Prompt 05 — Internal Linking Generator

You are an SEO content strategist.

Given these pages:

PILLAR:
[PILLAR URL/TITLE]

SUPPORTING PAGES:
[LIST OF TITLES/URLS]

For each page, recommend:
- 3–6 relevant internal links
- source section
- destination page
- natural anchor text
- reason the link helps the reader

Rules:
- Links must be contextually relevant.
- Do not recommend links just to increase the number of links.
- Avoid repetitive exact-match anchors.
- Prioritize links that move readers naturally through the topic cluster.
- Include a conversion/service page only when relevant.
