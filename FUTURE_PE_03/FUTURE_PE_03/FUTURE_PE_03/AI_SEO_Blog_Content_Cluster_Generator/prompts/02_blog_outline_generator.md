# Prompt 02 — SEO Blog Outline Generator

Create an SEO-focused blog outline for:

Primary keyword: [PRIMARY KEYWORD]
Secondary keywords/topics: [SECONDARY KEYWORDS]
Search intent: [INTENT]
Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Location: [LOCATION]
Business context: [BUSINESS CONTEXT]

Requirements:
- One clear H1.
- Logical H2 and H3 structure.
- Answer the search intent early.
- Include an introduction, main sections, FAQ where useful, and conclusion.
- Suggest natural places for internal links.
- Suggest a useful CTA without making the article overly promotional.
- Do not force keywords into every heading.
- Include factual claims only when they can be verified.
- Make the structure useful for humans first and search engines second.

Output:
1. Proposed title
2. Search intent
3. H1
4. H2/H3 outline
5. FAQ ideas
6. Internal-link opportunities
7. CTA suggestion
8. Editorial fact-check notes
