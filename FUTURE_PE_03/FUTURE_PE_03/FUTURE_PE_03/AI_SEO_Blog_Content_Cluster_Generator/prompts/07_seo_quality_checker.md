# Prompt 07 — SEO Quality Checker

Audit the following article as an SEO editor.

ARTICLE:
[PASTE ARTICLE]

Check:
1. Search intent match
2. Title clarity
3. H1 uniqueness
4. H2/H3 structure
5. Keyword naturalness
6. Topic coverage
7. Readability
8. Factual-risk areas
9. Internal-link opportunities
10. Local SEO naturalness
11. CTA relevance
12. Duplicate/repetitive sections
13. Unsupported claims
14. Meta title suggestion
15. Meta description suggestion

For every issue provide:
- Issue
- Severity: Critical / High / Medium / Low
- Why it matters
- Specific fix

Do not give an overall numerical SEO score. Give an actionable editorial checklist instead.
