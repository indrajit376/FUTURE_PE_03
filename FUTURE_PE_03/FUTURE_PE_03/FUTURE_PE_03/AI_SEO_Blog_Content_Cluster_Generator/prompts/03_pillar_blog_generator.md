# Prompt 03 — Pillar Blog Generator

Write a comprehensive pillar article for a business website.

BUSINESS:
[BUSINESS INFORMATION]

TOPIC:
[PILLAR TOPIC]

PRIMARY KEYWORD:
[PRIMARY KEYWORD]

LOCATION:
[LOCATION]

AUDIENCE:
[TARGET AUDIENCE]

GOAL:
[TRAFFIC / LEADS / EDUCATION / LOCAL VISIBILITY]

Create an original, useful long-form article.

Requirements:
- Clear H1 and H2/H3 hierarchy.
- Match the dominant search intent.
- Put the direct answer/value near the beginning.
- Use natural keyword variations rather than repetition.
- Explain concepts in plain language.
- Include practical decision-making information.
- Add a concise FAQ section when appropriate.
- Include 3–5 contextual internal-link placeholders.
- Include one clear but non-pushy CTA.
- Use local context only where genuinely relevant.
- Do not invent statistics, reviews, credentials, prices, medical claims or citations.
- If a claim requires professional verification, write [FACT-CHECK REQUIRED].
- Do not claim the article will rank or guarantee traffic.

Output in Markdown.
