### SEO Strategy

Instead of creating disconnected blog posts, I organized the content around one central topic:

**Dental Implants in Kolkata**

The supporting content answers narrower questions around:

• Cost factors  
• Treatment procedure  
• Aftercare  
• Implants vs dentures  

The goal is to create a useful content journey from informational search → deeper research → consultation.
