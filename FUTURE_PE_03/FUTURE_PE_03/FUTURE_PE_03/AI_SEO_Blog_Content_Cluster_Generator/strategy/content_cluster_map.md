## Cluster Logic

The pillar gives a broad overview and targets the main local topic.

Supporting articles answer narrower questions:

1. Cost → price expectations and factors.
2. Procedure → what treatment involves.
3. Aftercare → what happens after treatment.
4. Comparison → decision support between implants and dentures.

Each supporting article should link back to the pillar using a natural contextual anchor. The pillar should link to the most relevant supporting articles.
