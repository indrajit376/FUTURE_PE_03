## Intent Definitions

### Informational
The searcher wants to learn something. The page should answer the question clearly and avoid aggressive selling.

### Commercial Investigation
The searcher is comparing options, costs, providers or treatments. The page should explain decision factors and provide a clear next step.

### Transactional / Local
The searcher is close to taking action. The page should make contacting or booking the business easy.
