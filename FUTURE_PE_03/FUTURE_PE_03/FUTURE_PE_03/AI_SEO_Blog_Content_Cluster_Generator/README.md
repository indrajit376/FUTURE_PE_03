# AI SEO Blog & Content Cluster Generator for Business Websites

A complete AI-assisted SEO content system that generates search-intent-focused blog content, content clusters, local SEO variations, and internal-linking plans for business websites.

## Portfolio Project

**Example business niche:** Dental Clinic  
**Example location:** Kolkata, West Bengal, India  
**Primary business goal:** Generate qualified local search traffic and turn informational visitors into appointment enquiries.

> This is a demonstration SEO pack. Keyword volumes, difficulty scores, and rankings should be validated with current SEO tools before publishing or making performance claims.

## How the System Works

1. Define the business, location, audience and conversion goal.
2. Map keywords to search intent.
3. Choose one broad pillar topic.
4. Create supporting articles around specific subtopics.
5. Generate outlines before writing.
6. Write helpful, readable, business-focused content.
7. Add contextual internal links.
8. Adapt relevant pages for local search.
9. Run the SEO quality checklist.
10. Fact-check business, medical, legal and pricing claims before publishing.

## Important SEO Principle

AI should accelerate research, outlining and drafting—not replace editorial review. Avoid keyword stuffing, unsupported claims, fake statistics, fabricated citations, and repetitive content.

## Suggested Publishing Order

1. Pillar: Dental Implants in Kolkata

Then interlink the supporting articles to the pillar and to each other where genuinely useful.

## How to Use

Open any file in `prompts/`, copy the prompt into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, replace the variables in `[BRACKETS]`, and provide the required business information.

For production use, validate keywords and SERP competition with current SEO tools such as Ahrefs or Surfer SEO before final publication.
