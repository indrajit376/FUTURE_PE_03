# Dental Implant Procedure: What Happens Step by Step?

**Primary keyword:** dental implant procedure  
**Intent:** Informational / commercial investigation

## What Is the Dental Implant Procedure?

Dental implant treatment is a planned process that can involve assessment, implant placement, healing and restoration. The exact sequence depends on the individual case.

## Step 1: Consultation

The dentist reviews the missing tooth or teeth, oral health and relevant history. Diagnostic assessment may be recommended.

## Step 2: Treatment Planning

The clinician determines the appropriate treatment approach and discusses alternatives, expected stages and relevant considerations.

## Step 3: Implant Placement

Where implant treatment is appropriate, the implant component is placed according to the treatment plan.

## Step 4: Healing and Follow-Up

The treated area needs time and appropriate follow-up. Instructions will depend on the procedure performed.

## Step 5: Restoration

Once the appropriate treatment stage has been reached, the replacement tooth or restoration is fitted as planned.

## How Long Does the Process Take?

There is no single timeline for every patient. The number of stages, healing requirements and any additional procedures can affect treatment duration.

## How Should You Prepare?

Ask your dental professional for specific instructions. Bring relevant medical and dental information and tell the clinician about medications or conditions that may affect treatment.

## What Questions Should You Ask?

- What are my treatment options?
- What stages will my treatment include?
- What is the expected timeline?
- What are the alternatives?
- What follow-up will be required?
- What costs are included?

## FAQ

### Is dental implant placement painful?

Pain and discomfort vary between procedures and individuals. Your dental professional can explain what to expect and how discomfort is managed.

### Is every missing tooth treated the same way?

No. Treatment planning depends on the number and location of missing teeth and individual clinical factors.

### Can I decide based only on an online article?

Online content can help you prepare questions, but a qualified dental professional must assess your specific situation.
